Worship, day and night.

24-7 Worship is a vision for a Christian worship and prayer center in Vancouver: a place of healing, devoted to the one true living God — the Father, Jesus the Son, and the Holy Spirit.

Read the vision Get involved

01 — Vision

Set apart for the living God

This is a house for worship and prayer, still ahead of us, and offered in hope. When it opens, it opens for God, and for people who need a quiet place to meet him.

We worship one God: the Father, Jesus the Son, and the Holy Spirit. He is the only true and living God. Healing is something we ask of him, in sung praise and in prayer, for the person who comes hurting and for the city around the room.

The center is a place of worship. It is a room for the presence of God. People who need a physician, a counsellor, or urgent help should also seek that care. If someone is in immediate danger, call 911.

  • Worship

    Jesus at the center. Songs, Scripture, and thanksgiving offered to the Father, through the Son, in the Holy Spirit.

  • Prayer

    Intercession for Vancouver, for the churches, and for the people who walk through the door.

  • Healing

    A gentle room to be honest before God. We ask him to heal. We wait on him for what each night holds.

A different kind of night

The founder hopes to rent a venue, possibly near the Plaza of Nations, along False Creek, and to see that room become a place of worship. The hope is that hours often given to all-night parties, drinking, and drugs would be given to prayer and sung praise.

No venue has been rented. No street address is set. Nothing on this page is a confirmed location.

02 — How it works

One hour, then the next

Churches and worship teams sign up for hour-long slots. Each team leads worship and prayer, then hands the room to the team that follows, so praise has somewhere to continue.

  1. 01

    Claim an hour

    A church or worship team takes a single hour. The commitment is to arrive, lead, and stay long enough to welcome whoever comes next.

  2. 02

    Worship and pray

    Sing, read Scripture, and pray. A simple hour is enough. Silence and spoken prayer belong in the room with the songs.

  3. 03

    Hand it on

    The next team comes before the hour ends. The room stays occupied because another church has said yes to the hour after yours.

Where it begins

Weekend overnights. A stretch of hours through a Friday night or a Saturday night, while the circle of teams is still small. Dates will be shared when a venue is confirmed and teams have claimed hours. None are published here, because none are set.

Where it can grow

As more churches take part, those overnights can lengthen and multiply: weeknights as well as weekends, until worship and prayer continue twenty-four hours a day, seven days a week.

03 — Get involved

Come and keep watch

There is room for a team, a prayer, and a pair of hands. Expressing interest is free. This website does not take donations or payments.

  • Churches and worship teams

    Sign up for an hour. Lead sung worship and prayer, then leave the room in the care of the next team.

  • People who pray

    Come and intercede for the city, for the churches, and for whoever is in the room.

  • Practical help

    Welcome people, prepare the room, and tend the ordinary work that lets worship continue.
